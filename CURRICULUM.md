Little Steps curriculum coverage — reviewed 4 October 2026

This is an original English-medium multiple-choice practice bank for LKG, UKG and Classes 1–10. It covers core skills, not the entire CBSE syllabus, all subjects, textbook literature, experiments, art, PE or long-form writing. Preschool labels are local app levels (LKG=-1, UKG=0), informed by the NCERT foundational framework rather than a CBSE board-exam syllabus. Children in preschool need an adult to read prompts aloud and should also learn through real-world play and activities.

The live “Topics & curriculum references” disclosure lists the actual question counts, covered topics and official references for each class and subject. Questions are authored here; textbook exercises and literature have not been copied. Skills from earlier classes may recur as revision. School book editions and teaching order vary; the “Not studied yet” button records this distinction rather than calling an unstudied topic an incorrect answer.

Coverage:
- LKG/UKG: early counting, shapes, comparison, language/sounds, stories, self-care, surroundings and social skills.
- Classes 1–5: Mathematics, English skills, and The World Around Us (environmental learning).
- Classes 6–10: Mathematics, English skills, Science and Social Science.
- English: comprehension, vocabulary, grammar and recognition of writing conventions; no claim to full prescribed-literature or composition assessment.
- Class 9 uses the revised 2026–27 references (including sequences/progressions and current social-science themes), rather than assuming the previous syllabus is unchanged.

Sources support the choice of topic areas, not an endorsement of this app or a certification of exhaustive chapter coverage. Broader school-specific and language coverage needs the actual school's textbook list.

Content is maintained in scripts/build-curriculum.py; rebuilding it generates backend/data/curriculum.json. Each question includes a topic. On exam creation, ten questions are chosen across topics and frozen in that student's session. Weekly selections are stable within the week; practice varies. Replacements are selected server-side, exclude current questions and topics already marked unstudied, and are stored in the session. If the pool has no eligible topic left, the app explains this rather than looping. Marked topics are retained with the submitted attempt and visible in student/parent analytics. Replacement questions are graded normally; existing scores and sessions are preserved.
